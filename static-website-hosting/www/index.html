/ 01

A planted, predatory stance

Wide arches, frameless doors and a long low bonnet give the M8 a presence that reads as fast standing still. Carbon-fibre roof, quad tailpipes, and forged M wheels are not decoration — they are the spec sheet, made visible.

/ 02

A cockpit built around the driver

Merino leather, carbon trim and the red M1/M2 setup buttons on the wheel put track-day modes a thumb away. Two configurable presets swap throttle, damping, steering and all-wheel-drive bias in an instant — GT comfort to rear-drive fury.
